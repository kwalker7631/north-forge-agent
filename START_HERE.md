# Start here

You already have Google. You already have a chatbot in a browser.
That is not the problem.

The problem is a technician on a live call who needs a next step and
gets a paragraph that sounds finished. They give up. The machine is
still down. That is not stupidity. That is a tool that asked them to
be a prompt engineer while a customer is waiting.

North Forge is the opposite of that.

Plug in the drive. Double-click **Start North Forge**. Say the device,
the symptom, and what you already tried, in ordinary English. It is
supposed to answer like the go-to on the desk — and to say
“I do not have that page” instead of inventing one.

If you do not trust “AI,” good. Distrust is earned. A tool that hides
the hole is why people walk away. This one is built to show the hole.

It is for the person who never wants to see the word *model*.
It is for the expert tired of tools that move every six weeks.
It is for the book-smart person who can recite a manual and still
cannot reason through a paper path.
It is not a test.

## Why this is worth the years

The useful answer on this job is short and ordered:

1. What to look at first
2. What not to touch yet
3. What evidence to grab if it is still dead
4. What we still do not know

That takes compartmentalized TSC work — device, PC, network, PaperCut,
MyQ — sitting in procedures, not in a prompt nobody will write.

The long prompt already happened. You do not have to write it.
That is the point of the drive.

## Humanizer

No “Great question.”
No fake certainty.
No essay when you asked for a code.
A voice that sounds like someone who has been on the tools.

It learns what you just taught it. It is supposed to keep that.
That is the pocket brain: not a genius in the cloud, a go-to on the
desk that gets less wrong the longer the shop uses it.

## First four steps

1. Plug the drive in.
2. Double-click **Start North Forge**.
3. Type the problem the way you would tell a coworker.
4. If you get lost, type `/menu`.

Do not format the drive. Do not set up Python. Do not learn a vendor.
